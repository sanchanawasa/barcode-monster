SYSTEM / 01

HOW BARCODE MONSTER WORKS

SCAN

THE REAL WORLD IS THE SPAWN MAP

Instead of opening random digital packs, you physically hunt codes. A convenience store, supermarket, warehouse, library or kitchen can contain the next monster worth keeping.

DNA

ONE CODE, ONE PERMANENT CREATURE

A deterministic generator converts the barcode into a seed. That seed controls creature name, body shape, palette, rarity, HP, attack, defense, speed, element and special move.

DISCOVER

THE FUN IS FINDING WHAT NOBODY EXPECTED

A boring bottle of water could hide a monster better than a luxury product. Price and brand do not decide power. The digits do.

BATTLE

STATS BECOME GAMEPLAY

Your monster's speed, attack and defense matter in combat. A seemingly weaker monster can still win with better stat balance and turn order.

COLLECT

BUILD A DEX FROM THE THINGS AROUND YOU

Save your discoveries locally today. The long-term vision adds accounts, first-discovery records, world rarity tracking and competitive seasons.

SHARE

EVERY WEIRD FIND IS CONTENT

The best social loop is simple: scan something ordinary, reveal something absurdly rare, post the monster card, and send everyone hunting for the same product.

FAQ / SIGNAL ARCHIVE

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Does the same barcode always create the same monster?

Yes. The current generator is deterministic. The barcode creates a seed, and that seed creates the monster.

Are expensive products stronger?

No. Product price, brand and prestige are irrelevant. Only the barcode digits feed the current monster-generation system.

Can I scan a barcode with my phone camera?

Yes. Tap SCAN REAL BARCODE and permit camera access. The game supports common retail barcode formats with a browser-native scanner where available and a fallback scanner for other browsers.

Is my collection onchain?

Not in this Genesis build. Saved monsters and wins are currently stored locally in your browser. Global accounts and persistent discovery records are roadmap items.

Is $BCMON live?

No. The website currently treats $BCMON as a future Pump.fun concept and clearly labels it as coming soon.

Is this affiliated with product manufacturers?

No. Barcode Monster uses barcode numbers as deterministic game seeds and is not presented as an official project of the brands whose products players may scan.
